# "The Smile Mirror" by katahiromz

## What is this?

This is a camera app that uses facial recognition to create smiling faces and take photos or record videos.

## How to Use

* When you first launch the app, it will request camera and microphone permissions. Please allow these permissions.
* The camera will automatically launch and recognize faces as smiling faces.
* The three buttons on the bottom have the following meanings:
    - Camera Switch Button: Switches between the front and rear cameras.
    - Photo Button: Takes photos.
    - Record Button: Starts or stops recording.
* The gear in the upper right is the settings button. You can configure the app.

## Disclaimer

- Face recognition technology is currently not yet established, so it is theoretically impossible to consistently recognize faces correctly.
- Face recognition may fail in extremely bright, extremely dark, or sunny locations.
- Faces facing away from the camera, tilted faces, or sideways faces may not be recognized.
- Faces that are partially or completely off-screen or far from the camera may not be recognized.
- Faces with paint on them, faces wearing masks, faces from manga or anime, or animal faces may not be recognized.
- Faces that move violently or suddenly appear may not be recognized.
- If face recognition is not possible, the face may be displayed as is.
- This camera feature is optimized for streaming.
